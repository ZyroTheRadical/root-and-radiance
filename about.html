Who we are

Light for your plants. Soil for your soul.

Root & Radiance designs smart, self-watering planters for people who love green things but don't always remember to water them. Our flagship product, the Sprout Pod, quietly watches soil moisture, light and temperature, and waters your plant only when it actually needs it.

Every pod is designed in Regina, made from recycled ocean-bound plastic, and built to last for years, not seasons. We pair good hardware with an app that tells you, in plain language, what your plant is up to.

Our story

  • 2019 — Founders Omar Khorbatly and Hala Khorbatly kill their seventh basil plant in a single winter and decide something has to change.
  • 2020 — The first Sprout Pod prototype is built from a yogurt tub, a moisture sensor and a lot of hot glue.
  • 2022 — A crowdfunding campaign raises $410,000 from 6,200 backers across Canada.
  • 2024 — We open our Regina studio and start shipping to the United States.
  • Today — More than 85,000 plants are thriving in Sprout Pods across North America.

Our mission

To make every home a little greener by removing the guesswork from plant care, with products that are kind to the planet and easy to love.
